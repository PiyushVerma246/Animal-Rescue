## 📌 About AniCure

**AniCure** is an AI-based animal rescue and care management system designed to improve the way injured, abandoned, and distressed animals are reported, rescued, treated, and rehabilitated.

The platform brings together **AI-based injury analysis, geolocation, rescue coordination, real-time tracking, adoption, donations, rewards, and transparency** into a centralized system.

The goal is to make animal rescue operations **faster, better coordinated, transparent, and technology-driven**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional animal rescue processes often depend on phone calls, social media, and messaging platforms. This can result in:

- Delayed rescue responses
- Poor coordination between rescuers
- Difficulty finding nearby rescue teams
- Lack of intelligent case prioritization
- Duplicate rescue reports
- Limited rescue tracking
- Fragmented adoption processes
- Lack of transparency in donation management

AniCure addresses these challenges through a unified digital platform.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

AniCure provides a centralized platform where users can:

1. Report an injured or abandoned animal
2. Upload an image and location
3. Get AI-assisted injury assessment
4. Prioritize rescue cases based on severity
5. Notify nearby NGOs or rescue teams
6. Track rescue progress
7. Manage treatment and recovery
8. Support adoption and donations
9. Earn rewards for genuine contributions
10. Maintain transparent rescue and donation records

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🐕 Animal Reporting
- Report injured or abandoned animals
- Upload animal images
- Capture location
- Add relevant descriptions

### 🤖 AI-Based Injury Analysis
- Analyze uploaded animal images
- Detect possible injuries
- Assess condition
- Classify injury severity

### 📍 Geolocation-Based Rescue
- Detect reported animal location
- Identify nearby rescue resources
- Support faster rescue assignment

### 🚨 Priority & Notifications
- Classify cases as High, Medium, or Low priority
- Notify nearby rescue teams and NGOs

### 🚑 Rescue Tracking
- Accept rescue requests
- Track rescue progress
- Update rescue status
- Maintain rescue history

### 🏥 Treatment & Recovery
- Maintain treatment information
- Monitor recovery
- Continue treatment until recovery

### 🏠 Adoption & Donation
- List recovered animals for adoption
- Submit adoption requests
- Support animal welfare through donations

### 🎁 Reward System
- Reward genuine rescue reports
- Encourage community participation
- Promote active involvement in animal welfare

### 🔐 Transparency
- Maintain organized rescue and donation records
- Use blockchain-based mechanisms for transparent donation management
